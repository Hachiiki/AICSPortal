IMPORTANT FINDING, planning-estimate discrepancy (owner attention):
The Stage-2D planning report quoted option B3 as "rim #dcdddf, 12.6:1
vs topbar". Re-running the planning calculation script
(scripts/stage2d_contrast_calcs.py, outside the repo) shows its
oklab_blend() weights the FIRST color (the fill) by the alpha
parameter, so "white/15" was actually modeled as 85 percent white over
the fill. The CSS ring-white/15 is the opposite direction: 15 percent
white OVER the fill. The correctly-modeled expectation, and the
browser-measured reality:

  fill (dark --avatar-bg)     #2e333a   1.346:1 vs topbar (before)
  ring band (measured)        #4a4e54   2.045:1 vs topbar (after)
  ring band vs fill                     1.52:1
  theoretical 0.15*255+0.85*fill ~= #4c4e54 (the measured band matches
  to within AA rounding)

So the rendered edge definition improved from 1.346:1 to 2.045:1 — a
subtle, real lift — not to 12.6:1. Per the authorization ("if the ring
looks visually excessive or differs materially from the plan, stop and
report the finding instead of inventing another treatment"): the
authorized classes were implemented verbatim, nothing was substituted,
and the discrepancy is reported here rather than silently absorbed.
Visual inspection of the dark close-up (VLM review, focus/vlm/):
"very fine, crisp, well-defined boundary... subtle... does not appear
harsh, excessive, or halo-like... initials perfectly clear" — the
qualitative intent (a deliberate contour) is met. If the owner wants
the stronger rim magnitude the planning number implied, the follow-up
is a one-class alpha bump (e.g. dark:ring-white/30, roughly #74767c,
about 3.3:1 vs topbar) — NOT done here, awaiting owner decision.
